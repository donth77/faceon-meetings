## Motion

The moving backgrounds, in `motion/`. From one continuous shot of each source we took the section below and cross-faded its last seconds into its first, so it loops without a jump. Each was scaled to 1280 × 720 and saved as H.264 MP4 (High profile, level 3.1, no sound) of 3 MB at most, with a 320 × 180 WebP frame for the picker.
