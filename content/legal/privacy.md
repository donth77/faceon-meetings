{appName} is a free, open-source video meeting service. This policy explains what {appName} at {siteUrl} keeps about you, why, and for how long. {operator} runs this copy of {appName}. Anyone can run their own copy, and each copy has its own policy.

The short version: {appName} keeps only what it needs to run your meetings, has no ads and no tracking, doesn't record meetings, and never sells your information.

## What {appName} keeps

### Your account

- Your **name**, **email address** and **picture**. The picture is an avatar you pick, or your Google or GitHub photo if you choose it.
- Your **password**, if you set one, stored only as a secure hash, never as the password itself.
- Which **sign-in methods** you've connected. When you sign in with Google or GitHub, {appName} receives your name, email address and photo from them, and keeps the sign-in tokens they send, encrypted.
- Your **sign-in sessions**, with the IP address and browser they came from, so sessions can be checked and ended.

### Your meetings

- Meetings you create: the title, description and times, the meeting ID and passcode, the email addresses of people you invite, and your meeting settings.
- **Who joined and left**, with their names, pictures and times, so hosts and attendees can see past meetings under **Previous**.
- **AI notes a host chose to save**, with their transcript when it fits, and whether they're shared. Only whoever saved them and the meeting's host can see them, or, once shared, everyone who was in the meeting with an account.
- People in the **waiting room**, while they're asking to join.

### People without an account

Guests get a cookie with a random ID and avatar, so they're recognized when they come back. {appName} keeps the name a guest enters for as long as the meeting's records are kept.

### What {appName} doesn't keep

- **Audio and video** pass through the media service (below) to reach the other people in the meeting. They aren't recorded.
- **Chat messages** go straight to the people in the meeting and aren't stored. **Save chat** saves a copy on your own device only.
- **AI notes**, when a host turns them on: each person's speech is turned into text on their own device, and only the text goes to the host taking notes. The transcript and the notes stay in that host's browser tab; {appName} keeps them only if the host saves them (above). Anyone can choose not to be transcribed.
- **Settings on your device**, such as your microphone, camera and time format, stay in your browser.

## Why

To run the service: to sign you in, connect your meetings, show your upcoming and past meetings, send emails you ask for (like a password reset), and keep {appName} safe from abuse. Nothing is used for advertising or profiling.

## Cookies

{appName} uses only the cookies it needs to work: your sign-in session, a guest's cookie, and your language. There are no advertising or analytics cookies.

## Who else handles your information

{appName} relies on these services to run. Each handles only what it needs.

- **Vercel** hosts the website.
- **Neon** stores the database.
- **Stream** carries meeting audio, video, chat and reactions, and the text of AI notes to the host taking them, and keeps the name and picture each person uses in meetings.
- **The host's AI provider** (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, DeepSeek or OpenRouter), only when a host makes AI notes with their own AI key: the transcript goes from the host's browser straight to that provider, under the host's account with them.
- **Resend** sends account emails, such as verifying your address or resetting a password.
- **Google** and **GitHub**, only if you sign in with them.

## How long it's kept

- **Your account**, until you delete it. Deleting your account removes your account, your meetings and their records, and your profile at Stream, and ends any meeting you're hosting. Meetings you joined that other people host keep your name in who joined, until that's deleted after 90 days.
- **Instant meetings** are deleted after 30 days without use.
- **Who joined and left** is deleted after 90 days, and so are **saved AI notes**. Whoever saved them, or the meeting's host, can delete them sooner.
- **Waiting room requests and admission records** are deleted a week after the meeting.
- **Records of sign-in and join attempts**, kept to slow down password and passcode guessing, include your IP address and are deleted after a day.

## Your choices and rights

- **See and change** your name, picture and sign-in methods in **Settings**, under **Account**.
- **Delete** your account and its data at any time, from the same place.
- **Ask** for a copy of your information, or ask anything about this policy: {contact}

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to object to how your information is used, or to complain to a data protection authority.

## Children

{appName} isn't meant for children under 13, and accounts aren't for them.

## Security

Connections use HTTPS, passwords are hashed, sign-in tokens from Google and GitHub are encrypted, and meeting links carry their passcode in a part of the address that browsers never send to servers.

## Changes

When this policy changes, the new version appears here with a new date. Earlier versions are kept in {appName}'s code history.
