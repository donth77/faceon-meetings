## Is {appName} free?

Yes. {appName} is free and open source, with no ads and no tracking.

## Do I need an account?

You need one to host meetings. To join, you don't, as long as the host lets guests in.

## Are meetings recorded?

No. {appName} doesn't record meetings, and chat isn't saved after a meeting ends.

## What does {appName} keep about me?

Only what it needs to run your meetings, such as your account and who joined each meeting, and most of it only for a while. The [privacy policy](/privacy) lists everything that's kept and for how long, and the [terms](/terms) cover using {appName}.

## How long can a meeting last, and how many people can join?

It depends on how {appName} is set up for the host. Demo meetings have limits, and **Home** shows them to the host, with **Ask for full meetings** to ask this site's admins for more.

## Which browsers work?

Recent versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari, on a computer or a phone. Sharing your screen needs a computer. Sharing sound needs Chrome or Edge. A browser that can't join calls, or can't use your camera and microphone on the page, says so on the join screen.

## How do I find something in Help?

Type what you're looking for in **Search help**, at the top of every Help page, and pick an article. It opens with your words highlighted: use the arrows to go from one to the next, and **Clear** to remove the highlighting.

## Something's wrong. Where do I tell you?

Use **Found an issue?** on the [Help](/help) page to open a report on GitHub, where {appName}'s code lives.
