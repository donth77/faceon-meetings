With the waiting room on, people who arrive ask to join, and a host or co-host lets them in.

## Turn it on

- **For your personal room:** on the **Personal room** page, under **Room settings**.
- **For a scheduled meeting:** under **Security** when you schedule or edit it.
- **During a meeting:** in **Participants**, select **⋯**, then turn on **Waiting room**.

Turning it off during a meeting lets everyone who's waiting in.

## Let people in

When someone asks to join, you'll see a notice with their name. In **Participants**, under **Waiting to join**, select **Admit** or **Deny** for each person, or **Admit all**.

People you've admitted come straight back if they drop out and rejoin.

## Waiting to join

After selecting **Ask to join**, keep the page open. You'll go in as soon as you're admitted. If you're turned away, you can ask again, up to three times.
