## Schedule a meeting

1. Select **Schedule** on **Home**, or **Schedule a meeting** on **Upcoming**.
2. Give it a **Title**, then choose the **Date**, **Start time**, **Duration** and **Time zone**.
3. To make it repeat, choose an option under **Repeat**. **Custom** covers anything else, such as every other week or the last Friday of each month.
4. Under **Invite people**, add email addresses if you like. Under **Co-hosts**, add anyone who should join as a co-host (see [Host controls](/help/host-controls)); they're invited too.
5. With Google Calendar connected, leave **Add to my Google Calendar and send invitations** on (see below).
6. Under **Security**, choose whether it needs a passcode (keep the one shown, type your own, or select **Generate**), has a waiting room and lets guests in.
7. Under **When people join**, choose whether people join muted, can join before you, whether **AI notes start automatically**, and whether they're **shared with participants automatically** (once your meetings allow AI notes; see [AI notes](/help/ai-notes)).
8. Select **Save**.

Then copy the link or invitation, or add the meeting to Google Calendar, Outlook or any calendar app.

## Send invitations from Google Calendar

Connect Google Calendar once, and {appName} puts the meetings you schedule on it. Google emails each person you invite a real invitation they can accept from Gmail, Outlook or Apple Mail.

1. Go to **Settings**, then **Calendar**, and select **Connect Google Calendar**. You can also connect from the schedule form.
2. Sign in to Google with the account that uses your {appName} email, and allow {appName} to manage the events it makes.
3. When you schedule a meeting, **Add to my Google Calendar and send invitations** is on. Turn it off for a meeting you'd rather share yourself.

When you change or delete the meeting in {appName}, Google updates everyone's calendar and lets them know. Changing one meeting of a repeating series changes just that day; changing **This and following meetings** ends the series there and starts a new one, as Google Calendar does.

If Google Calendar couldn't be updated, the meeting is still saved: select **Retry**. If Google asks you to **Reconnect**, do it from the message or from **Settings**, then **Calendar**. {appName} only touches the events it makes.

## Find your meetings

**Upcoming** lists the next eight weeks of meetings you host or are invited to. Switch to the calendar to see a month or a week. Times show in your time zone, which you can change in [Settings](/help/settings).

## Change or delete a meeting

Open the meeting from **Upcoming** and select **Edit** or **Delete**. For a repeating meeting, choose what to change:

- **This meeting** only,
- **This and following meetings**, or
- **All meetings** in the series.

> **Tip:** A repeating meeting keeps the same link every time, so people can save it once.
