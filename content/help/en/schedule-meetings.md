## Schedule a meeting

1. Select **Schedule** on **Home**, or **Schedule a meeting** on **Upcoming**.
2. Give it a **Title**, then choose the **Date**, **Start time**, **Duration** and **Time zone**.
3. To make it repeat, choose an option under **Repeat**. **Custom** covers anything else, such as every other week or the last Friday of each month.
4. Under **Invite people**, add email addresses if you like.
5. Under **Security**, choose whether it needs a passcode, has a waiting room and lets guests in.
6. Select **Save**.

Then copy the link or invitation, or add the meeting to Google Calendar, Outlook or any calendar app.

## Find your meetings

**Upcoming** lists the next eight weeks of meetings you host or are invited to. Switch to the calendar to see a month or a week. Times show in your time zone, which you can change in [Settings](/help/settings).

## Change or delete a meeting

Open the meeting from **Upcoming** and select **Edit** or **Delete**. For a repeating meeting, choose what to change:

- **This meeting** only,
- **This and following meetings**, or
- **All meetings** in the series.

> **Tip:** A repeating meeting keeps the same link every time, so people can save it once.
