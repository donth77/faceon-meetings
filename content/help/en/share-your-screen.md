## Start sharing

1. Select **Share screen** at the bottom of the meeting.
2. In Chrome and Edge, choose what to share:
   - **A tab** is best for video and sound.
   - **A window** shares one app.
   - **Your entire screen** shares everything you see.
3. Pick what you want in your browser's window, then select **Share**.

In Firefox and Safari, your browser's own picker opens straight away.

## Share sound and video

To play a video with its sound, share **A tab** and leave **Share sound** on. Turn on **Optimize for video** for smooth motion. Leave it off for sharp text, like slides or code.

Chrome and Edge can share a tab's sound, and on Windows your entire screen's sound too. Firefox and Safari can't share sound.

## While you share

You see a preview of what everyone else sees. If you share your entire screen, the preview can show itself again and again, like facing mirrors. Select **Hide preview** to hide it for you only. Everyone else still sees your screen.

Select **Stop sharing** when you're done. You can also use `Alt+S` (`⌥S` on a Mac).

## One person at a time

When someone is already sharing, you'll see who. Hosts and co-hosts can select **Share instead** to take over. A host can also let only hosts share.

## On a phone

Phone browsers don't let websites share the screen. You can still see what others share.
