---
title: Previous meetings
summary: See past meetings, who came and for how long.
category: hosting
order: 4
---

**Previous** lists the meetings from the last 90 days that you hosted or joined while signed in.

- Point to someone's picture to see their name. Select **View details** to see who came, when they joined and left, and how long they stayed.
- Meetings with saved [AI notes](/help/ai-notes) you can read show **AI notes**, or **AI notes and transcript** when the transcript was saved with them. Select it to read them.
- In **View details**, **Download** offers what applies to that meeting:
  - **Attendance (CSV)**, for a meeting you hosted: a spreadsheet of who came, with each person's name, whether they joined as a guest, when they first joined and last left, and for how long. Email addresses appear only for the people you invited, and you.
  - **AI notes and transcript (Markdown)**, where there are notes you can read.
- **Download history** gives you all of it in one zip, like Google Takeout: a spreadsheet of every meeting here with your role, how long it lasted and how many came, the attendance of the meetings you hosted, and the AI notes you can read with their transcripts.
- Times in downloads are in your time zone. **Download your data**, in **Settings → Account**, also has your account and the meetings you schedule.
- Search by title or by a person's name.
- Choose **Hosted by me** to see only your own meetings.
- Select **Schedule again** to plan the same meeting for a new time.
