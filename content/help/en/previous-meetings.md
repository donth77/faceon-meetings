summary: See past meetings, who came and for how long.
category: hosting

---

**Previous** lists the meetings from the last 90 days that you hosted or joined while signed in.

- Select **View details** to see who came, when they joined and left, and how long they stayed.
- Meetings with saved [AI notes](/help/ai-notes) you can read show **AI notes**. Select **View details**, then **View AI notes**.
- Search by title or by a person's name.
- Choose **Hosted by me** to see only your own meetings.
- Select **Schedule again** to plan the same meeting for a new time.
