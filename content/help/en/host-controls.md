Whoever creates a meeting is its host, and the host can make others co-hosts. Co-hosts can do most of what a host can. Only the host can make or remove co-hosts, remove a co-host from the meeting, or end the meeting for everyone.

## Manage one person

Open **Participants**, or select **⋯** on someone's tile, to:

- **Mute** them, or **Ask to unmute**. Nobody can be unmuted without agreeing.
- **Stop video** or **Stop sharing**.
- **Pin for everyone**, to put them on everyone's main screen.
- **Make co-host** or **Remove as co-host**.
- **Remove from meeting**. They leave right away and can't rejoin.

## Mute everyone

Select **Mute all** at the bottom of **Participants**. Turn off **Let people unmute themselves** if people should raise a hand to speak.

## Meeting settings

In **Participants**, select **⋯** at the bottom:

- **Lock meeting:** nobody new can join.
- **Waiting room:** new people wait until a host lets them in. See [The waiting room](/help/waiting-room).
- **Allow chat** and **Allow reactions**.
- **Let people unmute themselves**.
- **Who can share their screen:** everyone, or hosts only.

## End the meeting

Select **Leave**. As the host, you choose **Leave meeting** to go while it continues, or **End meeting for all** to end it for everyone.
