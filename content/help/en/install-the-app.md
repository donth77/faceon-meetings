{appName} runs in your browser, so there's nothing you have to install. If you'd like it at hand, you can add it as an app: it opens in its own window, without the browser's address bar, and it's on your home screen, dock or taskbar like any other app. It's the same {appName}, with the same account and meetings.

## On a computer

- **Chrome or Edge:** select **Install app** in your account menu (your name, at the bottom left), or under **Settings**, **General**. You can also use the install icon at the end of the address bar.
- **Safari on a Mac:** in the menu bar, choose **File**, then **Add to Dock**.
- **Firefox** can't install apps. Chrome, Edge and Safari can.

## On a phone or tablet

- **iPhone and iPad:** tap **Share** (on newer iPhones it's in the **⋯** menu), then **Add to Home Screen**. This works in Safari, Chrome and Edge.
- **Android:** select **Install** on the card on **Home**, or **Install app** under **Settings**, **General**. You can also use the browser's menu: **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.

The card on **Home** shows once. If you put it away with **Not now**, **Settings** still has the option.

## Good to know

- Without a connection, the app says you're offline and tries again when you ask. Meetings need the internet.
- The app updates itself: each time it opens, it loads the latest {appName}.
- To remove it, remove it as you would any app: on a phone, press and hold its icon; on a computer, use **Uninstall** in the app's own menu, or remove it from the Dock.
