{appName} works in your browser, so you never need the app to join or host. The app for Mac and Windows is the same {appName} in a window of its own, plus what a browser tab can't do.

## What it adds

- **Meeting links open in the app.** See "Opening meeting links" below.
- **Mute from any app.** During a meeting, **Ctrl+Shift+A** (**⇧⌘A** on a Mac) mutes and unmutes while another app is in front. Change it, or turn it off, in **Settings → Desktop app → Mute shortcut**.
- **The tray or menu bar.** Start or join a meeting, and during one, mute or leave. Closing the window keeps {appName} there, unless you turn off **Keep {appName} in the tray** (**in the menu bar** on a Mac). To quit, choose **File → Quit {appName}** (**{appName} → Quit {appName}**, ⌘Q, on a Mac), or **Quit {appName}** in the tray icon's menu.
- **Google Calendar.** Google doesn't allow connecting it inside apps, so **Connect** opens your browser at Google's consent screen; {appName} notices when you're done.
- **Your computer's sound** when you share your screen, on Windows.
- **Reminders with no browser open**, at the time you chose in **Settings → Notifications**, with Join.
- **Unread chat** on the Dock or taskbar.
- **Updates itself**, quietly, and installs them when it restarts.

## Install it

On a Mac or a Windows PC, **Download for Mac** or **Download for Windows** (on the front page, in a one-time card on **Home**, in your account menu, or in **Settings → General**) gets the installer for your computer; so does the **Download** page, [/download](/download), which offers every build on other computers and links to all releases. On a Mac, open the `.dmg` and drag {appName} to Applications. On Windows, run the installer; it installs just for you, without asking for an administrator.

If your copy of the app isn't signed yet, your computer warns you the first time:

- **Mac:** right-click {appName} in Applications and choose **Open**, or go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**.
- **Windows:** in "Windows protected your PC", choose **More info → Run anyway**.

## Sign in

Email and password sign-in works in the app. **Continue with Google** and **Continue with GitHub** open your browser: sign in there, select **Continue as …**, and the app finishes signing you in. Signing out in the app leaves your browser signed in, and the other way round.

## Opening meeting links

A meeting link opens in your browser first. Once you've signed the app in from that browser, it knows the app is on this computer, and the join page offers **Open in the {appName} app**, beside **Join in this browser**. Tick **Always open meeting links in the app** to skip that choice.

You can change it in **Settings → General → Open meeting links in the desktop app**, on that browser: **Ask each time**, **Always** or **Never**. With **Always**, the join page opens the app at once, with **Join in this browser** in case it doesn't.

A link's passcode goes with it to the app, without passing through any server.

## On a Mac

The first time you share your screen, macOS asks you to allow screen recording in **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen Recording**. Turn {appName} on there, then quit and reopen {appName}.

## Uninstall

- **Mac:** quit {appName} from the menu bar, then drag it from Applications to the Trash.
- **Windows:** **Settings → Apps → Installed apps → {appName} → Uninstall**.
