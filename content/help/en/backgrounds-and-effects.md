Blur what's behind you, or put a picture there instead. {appName} finds you in each frame on your own device, so nobody ever receives your video without the effect, and nothing about it is sent anywhere.

## Choose one before you join

On the screen before you join, select **Backgrounds and effects** on your preview. The meeting starts with your choice, and the preview says **Background on** while one is chosen.

## Change it in a meeting

Select the arrow on the camera button, then **Backgrounds and effects…**. On a phone, it's in **More**. The panel shows your video as others see it, and a choice applies at once.

## What you can choose

- **No effect**, **Slight blur** or **Blur**.
- **Art**: four public-domain paintings from The Metropolitan Museum of Art.
- **Places**: a library, a valley, Earth from space and a coastline, all public domain.
- **Abstract**: four soft gradients.
- **Motion**: Aurora, Waves, Lights and Drift, which move gently. Subtle motion looks best, and uses less data.
- **Yours**: select **Add** to use your own image (JPEG, PNG, WebP or GIF, up to 10 MB) or a looping video (MP4 or WebM, up to 60 seconds and 50 MB). Images are cropped to 16:9. What you add stays in this browser and is never uploaded. Select **×** on one to remove it.

Your choice is kept on this device and used in every meeting. **Settings**, under **Backgrounds and effects**, sets it outside a meeting.

## If your device can't keep up

Finding you in every frame takes some work. If your device can't keep up, {appName} first does less of it, then turns effects off to keep your video smooth, and tells you. Choosing a background again tries once more.

Backgrounds and effects need a recent Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. Where a browser can't run them, the button doesn't appear.

## Good to know

- Your video is mirrored to you if you like it that way (**Settings**, under **Video**), so text in a background reads backwards to you only. Everyone else sees it the right way round.
- The first time, your browser downloads what it needs to find you, a few megabytes, and then keeps it.
- For the cleanest edges, light your face and sit in front of something that stands out from your hair and clothes. {appName} lines its outline up with what your camera sees, so a dark sweater in front of a dark wall blurs where they meet.
