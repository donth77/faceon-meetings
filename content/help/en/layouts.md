## Choose a layout

Select **Layout** at the top right of the meeting:

- **Auto** picks for you. In a call with one other person, they fill the screen and you're in the corner. With more people, everyone shares the screen. A shared screen takes the main spot.
- **Gallery** shows everyone at the same size.
- **Speaker** shows whoever is talking, large.

## Pin someone

Select **⋯** on a person's tile, then **Pin for me**. They stay large on your screen until you unpin them. Nobody else sees the change.

Hosts and co-hosts can select **Pin for everyone** to put someone on everyone's main screen.

## See who's talking

The person speaking gets a green outline, and green bars beside their name move with their voice. When their camera is off, a ring pulses around their picture.

## Full screen

Select **More**, then **Full screen**. Press `Esc` to leave full screen.
