## Choose a layout

Select **Layout** at the top right of the meeting:

- **Auto** picks for you. In a call with one other person, they fill the screen and you're in the corner. With more people, everyone shares the screen. A shared screen takes the main spot.
- **Gallery** shows everyone at the same size.
- **Speaker** shows whoever is talking, large, with everyone else beside them.
- **Spotlight** shows only whoever is talking, or the shared screen.

In the same menu, **Hide self view** hides your own tile from you (others still see you), and **Hide tiles without video** leaves out people whose camera is off.

## Pin someone

Select **⋯** on a person's tile, then **Pin for me**. They stay large on your screen until you unpin them. Nobody else sees the change.

Hosts and co-hosts can select **Pin for everyone** to put someone on everyone's main screen.

## See who's talking

The person speaking gets a green outline, and green bars beside their name move with their voice. When their camera is off, a ring pulses around their picture.

## Keep the meeting in view

In Chrome and Edge on a computer, select **More**, then **Open picture-in-picture**. A small window stays on top of your other tabs and apps, showing whoever is talking or the shared screen, with **Mute**, **Video**, **Raise hand** and **Leave**.

Chrome can also open it by itself when you switch to another tab or app during a meeting, including when you share one. It asks you the first time. To stop it, turn off **Open picture-in-picture automatically** in **Settings**, under **Video**.

## Full screen

Select **More**, then **Full screen**. Press `Esc` to leave full screen.

## On a phone

The video fills the screen, and the controls step aside a few seconds after you last touch them. Tap the video to bring them back, and tap it again to send them away.

They stay while a menu or panel is open, while you share your screen, and while you're the only one here. While they're away, a small strip at the top shows if your mic is off, your hand is up, AI notes are on or time is running out.

With a keyboard, any key brings them back. With a screen reader, select **Show meeting controls**: they then stay until you tap the video.
