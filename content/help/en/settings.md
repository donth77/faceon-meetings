Open **Settings** at the bottom of the sidebar. During a meeting, select **More**, then **Settings**. Changes save as you make them and apply on this device. Your account settings follow you everywhere.

## General

- **Language**: {appName}'s language.
- **Time format**: 12-hour or 24-hour times.
- **Time zone**: the zone meeting times show in. It starts as your device's.
- **Theme**: light, dark, or matching your device. The meeting room is always dark, so video stands out.

Dates follow your browser's region, so you'll see "Sunday 1 November" or "Sunday, November 1", whichever you're used to.

## Audio

Choose your microphone and speaker, watch the microphone meter, and select **Test speaker** to hear a short sound. **Noise suppression** filters out typing, fans and traffic. **Push to talk** lets you hold `Space` to talk while muted; it's off unless you turn it on.

## Video

See your camera, choose which one to use, and decide whether to mirror your video, send HD video, or start meetings with your camera off.

## Reactions

Choose whether others' reactions show (yours always do), whether reactions float up the screen, whether a soft sound plays with each, and what screen readers hear: nothing, a short summary at most every 10 seconds, or each reaction.

## Notifications

Choose which events make a sound (people joining and leaving, chat messages, raised hands, the waiting room) and which show a pop-up.

**Meeting reminders** remind you 5, 10 or 15 minutes before meetings you host or are invited to, while FaceOn is open in a tab. Allow notifications to get them in any tab. Calendar files you add from a meeting carry the same reminder, so your calendar reminds you even when FaceOn is closed.

## AI notes

How AI notes are made: **On this computer**, with Chrome's built-in AI, or with **Your own AI key** from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, DeepSeek or OpenRouter. Your key stays in this browser. See [AI notes](/help/ai-notes).

## Accessibility

{appName} follows your device for reduced motion, contrast and text size. These settings cover what your device can't know:

- **Single-key shortcuts**, such as `?` for the list of shortcuts, and holding `Space` to talk when Push to talk is on. Turn them off if you use speech input or a switch device.

## Account

Your picture, name, sign-in methods and password. See [Your account](/help/your-account).
