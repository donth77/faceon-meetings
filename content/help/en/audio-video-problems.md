If {appName} says this browser can't use a camera or microphone on this page, open the meeting link in a recent Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. Some apps' built-in browsers can't use them, and no browser can on a page that isn't secure (its address doesn't start with `https`).

## Nobody can hear me

1. Check that **Mute** isn't red.
2. Select the arrow beside it and pick the microphone you're using.
3. Open **More**, then **Settings**, and watch the meter under **Audio** while you talk. If it doesn't move, your browser or computer may be blocking the microphone.
4. To unblock it, select the icon at the left of the address bar and allow the microphone. Then check your computer's privacy settings:
   - **Windows:** Settings › Privacy & security › Microphone.
   - **Mac:** System Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone.

## I can't hear anyone

- Check your computer's volume, and that the right speaker or headphones are chosen. Select the arrow beside **Mute** to switch.
- Select **Test speaker** in **Settings**, under **Audio**, to play a short sound.

## My camera doesn't work

- **It says it's in use:** another app, often another meeting app, is using it. Close that app, then turn your camera on again.
- **It's blocked:** allow the camera from the icon in the address bar, and in your computer's privacy settings, as for the microphone.

## People hear an echo

Wear headphones, or move away from your speakers. Keep **Noise suppression** on **Strong** or **Standard**.

## Video is choppy, or it says "Reconnecting"

- Close other tabs and apps that use the internet heavily.
- Turn off **Send HD video** in **Settings**, under **Video**.
- If it says **Reconnecting**, {appName} is getting you back. Stay on the page.
