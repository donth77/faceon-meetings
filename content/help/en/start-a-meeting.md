You need an account to host. Anyone can join, and people without an account can join too if you allow guests.

## Start one now

1. On **Home**, select **New meeting**.
2. Select **Start an instant meeting**.
3. Check your camera and microphone in the preview, then select **Join now**.

You're in. To bring people in, see [Invite people](/help/invite-people).

## Make a link for later

1. On **Home**, select **New meeting**.
2. Select **Create a meeting for later**.
3. Select **Copy link** and send it to the people you want to meet with.

The link works whenever you're ready. Open it yourself to start the meeting.

## Schedule it

Select **Schedule** on **Home** to pick a date and time, make it repeat, and invite people by email. See [Schedule and repeat meetings](/help/schedule-meetings).

## Use your personal room

Your personal room has a link that never changes, which is handy for regular calls. See [Your personal room](/help/personal-room).

> **Demo meetings.** Some accounts host demo meetings, with a limit on time and people. When yours are demos, **Home** shows the limits.
