Go to **Settings**, then **Account**.

## Picture and name

People see your picture when your camera is off.

- Pick one of the avatars, or select **Shuffle** for new ones. You can also change it as you join a meeting: select your avatar beside your name.
- If you signed in with Google or GitHub, you can use that photo instead.
- Change your **Display name**. It's the name people see in meetings.

## How you sign in

You can sign in with Google, GitHub, or your email and a password, and use more than one.

- **Connect** Google or GitHub. It must use the same email address as your account.
- **Remove** a method you no longer use. You always keep at least one.
- Connecting Google Calendar (**Settings**, then **Calendar**) also connects Google as a way to sign in. Removing Google disconnects Google Calendar too.
- **Set a password** to sign in with your email, too, or **Change password**. Changing it signs you out on your other devices.

## Download your data

Select **Download your data** for a zip of what {appName} keeps about you that you can see: your account, the meetings you host with their settings and invitees, who came to the meetings you hosted or joined in the last 90 days, the AI notes you can read, and your requests to host full meetings. It's JSON, with spreadsheet (CSV) copies and your AI notes as Markdown, and a README that says what's in it. Chat, audio and video aren't in it, because {appName} never stores them. Settings kept on your device stay in your browser.

## Delete your account

Select **Delete account**. This removes your meetings, notes and avatar for good, and people you invited can no longer join your meetings. If you connected Google Calendar, your upcoming meetings are cancelled there first, so Google lets the people you invited know, and then {appName} gives up its access to your calendar. Past meetings stay in it. If you haven't signed in recently, you'll be asked to sign in again first.

The [privacy policy](/privacy) explains what's deleted with your account, and what's kept for a while.
