Your personal room has a **Personal Meeting ID** and link that stay the same, so people can save them and use them any time you meet.

## Start it

Select **Start** on **Home**, or on the **Personal room** page in the sidebar.

## Share it

On the **Personal room** page, under **Join details**, you can copy the Personal Meeting ID, the passcode or the invite link. **Copy invitation** copies all of them as a ready-made message. Select **Change** next to the passcode to choose your own (see [Invite people](/help/invite-people)).

## Room settings

- **Waiting room:** people wait until you or a co-host let them in.
- **Let guests join without an account:** guests only need the link and a name.
- **Mute people when they join:** they can unmute themselves.
- **Co-hosts:** people who join your room as co-hosts when signed in with these email addresses (see [Host controls](/help/host-controls)).

## AI notes

Once your meetings allow AI notes (see [AI notes](/help/ai-notes)), your room has its own **AI notes** section:

- **Start AI notes automatically:** AI notes start as you or a co-host joins.
- **Share AI notes with participants automatically:** notes made in your room are saved with it and shared as soon as they're made.

## Get a new ID

If you've shared the link too widely, select **Regenerate ID**. The old link and passcode stop working right away, so send people the new invitation.
