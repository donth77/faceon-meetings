---
title: Keyboard shortcuts
summary: Both Meet-style and Zoom-style keys work.
category: account
order: 3
---

Press `?` anywhere to see these, or `Ctrl+/` (`⌘/` on a Mac). They're also listed in **Settings**, under **Accessibility**, including in a meeting's Settings.

## In a meeting

| Action | Windows and Linux | Mac |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mute or unmute | `Ctrl+D` or `Alt+A` | `⌘D` or `⌥A` |
| Talk while muted (with **Push to talk** on, in **Settings**, under **Audio**) | Hold `Space` | Hold `Space` |
| Start or stop video | `Ctrl+E` or `Alt+V` | `⌘E` or `⌥V` |
| Share your screen or stop sharing | `Alt+S` | `⌥S` |

## Anywhere

| Action | Windows and Linux | Mac |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Show or hide the sidebar | `Ctrl+B` | `⌘B` |
| Show keyboard shortcuts | `?` or `Ctrl+/` | `?` or `⌘/` |

Shortcuts don't fire while you're typing in a box.

If you use speech input or a switch device, turn off **Single-key shortcuts** in **Settings**, under **Accessibility**. Then `Space` and `?` do nothing, and `Ctrl+/` (`⌘/`) still shows this list.
