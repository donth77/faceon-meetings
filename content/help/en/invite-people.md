## During a meeting

- Select the shield icon beside the meeting's name, at the top left. Then select **Copy link** or **Copy invitation**.
- Or open **Participants** and select **Invite**.

While you're the only one there, a card in the corner shows the link too.

## Before a meeting

- **Scheduled meetings:** open the meeting from **Upcoming** to copy the link or invitation, or to add it to Google Calendar, Outlook or any calendar app. With Google Calendar connected, Google emails the invitations for you when you save the meeting. See [Schedule and repeat meetings](/help/schedule-meetings).
- **Your personal room:** select **Copy invitation** on **Home** or on the **Personal room** page.

## About the passcode

The passcode is part of the link, so people with the link don't need to type it. If you share only the meeting ID, share the passcode too. The invitation includes both.

> **Tip:** An invitation is plain text, so you can paste it into an email, a chat or a calendar event.
