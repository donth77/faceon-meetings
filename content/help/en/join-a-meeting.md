## Join from a link

1. Open the meeting link you were sent.
2. In the preview, check that people will see and hear you:
   - Turn your microphone and camera on or off with the buttons on the preview.
   - Choose a **Microphone**, **Speaker** and **Camera** below it.
   - Say something. When the meter moves, it says **Your mic is working**.
   - Select **Play a test sound** to check your speaker.
3. Enter your name if you're not signed in.
4. Select **Join now**.

To join without turning anything on, select **Join without audio and video**.

## Join with a meeting ID

On **Home**, or on the sign-in page if you don't have an account, enter the 10-digit meeting ID or paste the link under **Meeting ID or link**, then select **Join**.

If the meeting has a passcode, you'll be asked for it. Links usually include the passcode, so you won't need to type it.

## If you have to wait

- **The host hasn't started yet.** You'll join as soon as they do. Keep the page open.
- **The meeting has a waiting room.** Select **Ask to join**. The host or a co-host lets you in.
- **The meeting is locked or full.** Ask the host, then try again.

## Joining without an account

If the host allows guests, all you need is the link and a name. Sign in instead if you'd like the meeting to show in your **Previous** list.
