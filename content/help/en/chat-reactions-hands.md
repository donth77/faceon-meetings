## Chat

Select **Chat** to open it. Messages go to everyone in the meeting.

- When chat is closed, new messages pop up briefly, and the **Chat** button shows how many you haven't read.
- Chat isn't saved after the meeting ends. To keep a copy, select the download icon at the top of the chat. It saves the messages as a text file.
- On a computer, **Participants** and **Chat** can be open at the same time.

## Reactions

Select **Reactions** and pick 👍, ❤️, 😂, 😮, 👏 or 🎉. Your reaction floats up the screen and shows on your tile for a few seconds.

## Raise your hand

Select **Raise hand**. A hand shows on your tile, and hosts see you in the **Raised hands** list, in the order hands went up. Select **Lower hand** when you're done.

Hosts and co-hosts can lower anyone's hand, or select **Lower all hands**.

> **Hosts can turn chat and reactions off.** When they do, you'll see a note, and only hosts can still send them.
