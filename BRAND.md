# The FaceOn name and logo

FaceOn is open source under the [MIT License](LICENSE): you're welcome to run it, change it and share it. Keep the license with the code, as it asks.

One request, about the name and logo. If you run a copy that other people use, give it its own name and logo, or say plainly that it's based on FaceOn, as in "Acme Meet, based on FaceOn". That way nobody mistakes your copy for the one the FaceOn project runs, and questions about yours reach you.

- Changing the name, logo and colors takes a few minutes: see [Make it yours](DEPLOY.md#make-it-yours).
- Saying where your copy came from is welcome. Please don't suggest that the FaceOn project runs or endorses it.
- A copy just for you, or for trying changes, can keep the name.

This is a request, not part of the license.
