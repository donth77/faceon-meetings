# FaceOn for Windows and Mac

The desktop app is FaceOn in a window of its own, plus a few things a browser tab can't do:

- **Meeting links open in the app.**
- **Mute from any app** with **Ctrl+Shift+A** (**⇧⌘A** on a Mac), even when another app
  is in front.
- **The tray or menu bar:** start or join a meeting, and mute or leave during one.
- **Reminders** before your meetings, even with no browser open.
- **It updates itself.**

You never need it to join or host a meeting: FaceOn works in your browser too.

## Download

Get it at [joinfaceon.app/download](https://joinfaceon.app/download). There's no Linux
version yet.

## Install

- **Windows:** run `FaceOn-Setup.exe`. It installs just for you, without asking for an
  administrator. Until the app is signed, Windows may say it "protected your PC": select
  **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **Mac:** open `FaceOn.dmg` and drag FaceOn to Applications.

## Sign in

Email and password work in the app. **Continue with Google** and **Continue with GitHub**
open your browser: sign in there, and the app finishes signing you in.

## Uninstall

- **Windows:** **Settings → Apps → Installed apps → FaceOn → Uninstall**.
- **Mac:** quit FaceOn from the menu bar, then drag it from Applications to the Trash.
